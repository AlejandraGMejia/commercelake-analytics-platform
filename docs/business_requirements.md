# Business Requirements

## Business context

CommerceLake represents a fictional retail company
operating through multiple sales channels.

## Business objectives

1. Centralize retail transaction data.
2. Monitor revenue and order performance.
3. Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
4. Measure product and category performance.
5. Improve data reliability and accessibility.

## Initial analytical questions

- How does revenue change over time?
- Which categories generate the most revenue?
- What is the average order value?
- What percentage of customers make repeat purchases?
- How does sales performance differ by channel?

## Data limitations

Source datasets have not yet been selected.

Margin, inventory, and channel analysis will only
be implemented if the selected data sources
support those metrics reliably.

## Success criteria

- Documented business definitions
- Validated analytical models
- Reproducible ingestion
- Automated critical data quality checks
- Documented end-to-end pipeline
